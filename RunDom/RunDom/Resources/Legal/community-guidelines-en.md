# Community Guidelines

**Last updated:** May 28, 2026
**Version:** 1

Runpire is a community of people who love running. To keep everyone safe and respected, we ask all users to follow these guidelines.

## 1. Be respectful

Treat other users with respect in all interactions. Harassment, insults, hate speech, discrimination and threats are strictly forbidden. This includes content targeting gender, race, religion, nationality, sexual orientation or physical attributes.

## 2. Be authentic

Post as yourself. Impersonating others, creating fake accounts or sharing misleading information is forbidden. This includes manipulating running stats (e.g. GPS spoofing, uploading fake routes).

## 3. No inappropriate content

The following content is not allowed:

- Nudity or sexual content
- Violence, blood, or injury imagery
- Copyrighted material you don't own rights to
- Content promoting illegal activities
- Content glorifying extreme unhealthy behaviors (eating disorders, self-harm)

## 4. No spam

Don't post repetitive, irrelevant, commercial or manipulative content. Reposting the same content, copy-pasting comments, buying followers and similar behaviors are forbidden.

## 5. Respect others' privacy

- Don't share another person's location, home or work address
- Don't share photos or videos of others without consent
- Don't share other people's personal information (phone, email, address)

## 6. Run safely

Runpire is a game, but you're running in the real world. Watch for traffic, don't take risks in dangerous areas, don't put others' safety at risk. Don't attempt runs that aren't appropriate for your age or health condition.

## Consequences for violations

Content that violates these guidelines may result in:

1. **First violation:** Content removal and a warning to the user
2. **Repeated violations:** Temporary account suspension
3. **Severe or repeated violations:** Permanent account deletion

To report content that violates these rules, use the **"..." menu on the post → "Report"**. Our team reviews every report.

## Contact

If you encounter an issue or want to provide feedback about these guidelines, use the in-app reporting flow.
